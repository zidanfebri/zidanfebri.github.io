Academic Information System

SIAKAD SMA El Fitra

The school's academic data hub. Teachers record attendance, journals, and grades online, and homeroom teachers get grade ledgers and report cards without manual compiling. The other applications (the exam portal and the LMS) pull student, class, and schedule data from here through a REST API.

  • Roles for admin, teacher, homeroom teacher, finance, counselor, and principal
  • Class schedules, class and subject attendance, teaching journals
  • Daily/midterm/final grade entry, grade ledgers, rankings, and PDF report cards
  • Qur'an memorization (ziyadah) with monthly reports and digital signatures
  • Student biodata, family card & birth certificate uploads, and parent portal accounts
  • Teacher and staff sick/out-of-office requests, plus tuition (SPP) management

Online Exams & LMS

Exam & Learning Portal

Used for daily quizzes, midterms, final exams, and tryouts. Students take exams through Safe Exam Browser or the school's Android app, proctors monitor each room live, and graded scores are sent automatically to report cards in SIAKAD.

  • Question bank with multiple choice, essay, and table questions; Excel import; math formula editor
  • Detection of leaving the tab, logging in on another device, and pasted text, with durations
  • Mixed exam rooms across classes, seat numbers, and per-session proctors assigned by the committee
  • Live room monitor: open/close access, reset time, make-up exams
  • Online learning modules and assignments, notifications, and a parent portal
  • Touch-screen student council (OSIS) election voting secured with a PIN
